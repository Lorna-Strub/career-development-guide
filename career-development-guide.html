Technical Skills

Technical skills vary widely by role, department, and function across Oshkosh. The list below is not all-encompassing — it's a starting point to help you identify and search for the specific technical skills relevant to your job and career goals.

Finding Technical Skills on LinkedIn Learning

Because technical skills differ so much from role to role (engineering, manufacturing, IT, quality, supply chain, finance, and more), the best way to find content specific to your job is to search LinkedIn Learning directly:

  • Log in to LinkedIn Learning through Workday
  • Use the search bar to enter your specific skill, tool, or software (e.g., "AutoCAD," "Six Sigma," "Excel formulas," "SQL," "Lean Manufacturing")
  • Filter results by skill level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced) to match where you're starting from
  • Save courses to a learning collection so you can track your progress over time
